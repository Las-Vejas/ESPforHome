# October 24, 2026: Made the schematic
<!-- fabricate:entry 1201 -->

After buying a 3d printer and setting up a homelab, i realised that sensors are super cool, and that i love iot, so thats why I want to make a super cool ESPHome optimised board. 

I'm making this board as a simple and cheap board (aiming for <10$ a piece). So i will have esp332-c6-mini-1, power stuff, and then just pin headers.

I have most of the parts (resistors, caps, esp32's, etc...) so i'm making this so i could lowk mass produce it for myself lol. 

I'm using the AP2112K-3.3 for the voltage regulator. This board will have 14 GPIOs that you can use, along with 2 GND pins, one 3V3 pin and a 5V pin. 

The board will be 30mm x 30mm so reallly tiny, and the back will be completely empy so you could make sensors with very small sizes.

# October 24, 2026 (entry 2): Routed the PCB
<!-- fabricate:entry 1219 -->

So this board has to be very small, and i mean TINY! So i made this board 30 x 30 mm big, with 3 mounting holes. I put 2 9-pin headers on each side. I'm kinda surprised everything fit on this tiny board. I made a cutout for the antenna, and rounded all the corners so that it would look polished. There are 3 mounting holes for M2 screws. I'm very proud of this board.
